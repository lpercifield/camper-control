# NNNN - Short imperative title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Accepted | Superseded by NNNN

## Context

What was true that forced a choice. The constraint, not the story.

## Decision

What we do. Present tense, one paragraph.

## Alternatives rejected

What a reasonable person would have done instead, and why it was not done.
This is the part that stops the decision being quietly reversed.

## Consequences

What this costs, and what will make it worth revisiting.
